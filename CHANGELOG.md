# Changelog

## 1.12 — 2026-11-28

- **Practice words** (new, on the home screen): active practice with your own words, ten questions at a time — *Write the word* (the translation is shown, you write the word; one small typing mistake is shown but counts), *der · die · das*, *Verb forms* (ich ___ · gehen · Perfect → bin gegangen), and *Say it*: say the word aloud and the phone tells you whether it understood it (what it heard is shown; listen and try again). With few saved words, common words of your level fill in.
- **Progress** (new): your streak (days in a row with some learning) with the last seven days, the best streak, this week's answers, right answers in percent, words saved and learned, and charts of the last eight weeks: words learned and answers given. Reviews, practice and saved words all count.
- **Widget**: the clipboard button did the same as the search bar (both opened the translate window, which already has Paste). It is now *Review saved words*.

## 1.11 — 2026-11-27

- **Widget, fixed**: after an update the home screen kept the old widget, where every tap opened the app on the last search. The widgets are now redrawn as soon as the app is updated.
- **Widget words open right there**: tapping a recent word (or the word on the Word card) shows its translation in the small window over the home screen, not the whole app; *Open in Trilingo* in that window shows everything about it.

## 1.10 — 2026-11-27

- **Example sentences for every tense**: in Conjugation each tense (Present, Simple past, Perfect, Future, Imperative …) has an *Examples* button: real sentences that use that tense of the verb, the verb forms in bold, with translations and audio. Sentences of another tense that share a word (خواهیم رفت is future, not the past رفت) are left out.
- **The keyboard stays closed**: closed with Back right after the app opens, it no longer comes back up (it used to, twice).
- **Widget**: the search bar now opens a small translate window right on the home screen (type, see the translation, without leaving it); under it, the *word of the day* — one of your saved words with its translation, a new one each day.

## 1.9 — 2026-11-27

- **Did you mean, much better**: up to two or three typing mistakes are understood, not one. Typical mistakes count less: a neighbouring key, a missing or doubled letter, swapped letters, letters that sound the same (ph/f, ä/e, schoen → schön; in Persian س/ص/ث, ز/ذ/ض/ظ, ت/ط, ه/ح, ق/غ, spoken آسمون → آسمان), a left-out vowel. Among close words the more common one wins. *Other spellings* shows the next best ones; a sentence gets each misspelt word fixed (how ar you todya → how are you today). Also offered when the service translated a misspelt word anyway, and without internet (the built-in dictionary's entry for the corrected word).
- **Widget**: beside the search bar, a microphone (speak a word), *Translate copied text* (in a small window over any app) and *Scan text* (opens the camera).
- **New widget: Word card** — review your saved words on the home screen: Show, then Again or Got it, exactly as in Review. When nothing is due, it goes through your saved words; with none saved, your recent words. Settings → Add Word card to home screen.
- **Scan text, much more**: drag over the photo to choose some lines (shown, translated, copied or read aloud); the whole text under the photo — correct a misread word, translate the whole text, copy it, hear it; a tapped word can be heard and saved with the star; words above your level are outlined in orange, saved words in green; *Turn photo* for a sideways picture.

## 1.8 — 2026-11-26

- **A meaning for every search**: when a word has no dictionary entry of its own, the meaning section shows the closest help instead of staying empty — the meaning of its dictionary form (ging → gehen, Häuser → Haus, aufgestanden → aufstehen, رفتم → رفتن), the Wikipedia summary for names (Berlin, EU), or for a sentence the meanings of its words. Always in the word’s own language: never the meaning of a translation. A line above it says what is shown.
- **Numbers**: a number typed or spoken as digits (8, ۸, 1.000, 3,5, 8.) is written out in all three languages (eight, acht, هشت) with its ordinal, a meaning and a picture to count. Spoken in Persian, "هشت" no longer stays "8".
- **German compound words** (Marketingassistentin, Kinderarztpraxis): recognised as German, a typo in one part is fixed (marketingassitentin → Marketingassistentin), the parts are shown with their translations (tap one to look it up), "Female form of …" for -in words, an offline translation from the parts, and the picture and meaning of the last part when the whole word has none. Saved and reviewed with the corrected spelling, and accepted in *Words from a text* and Review.
- **Abbreviations** are written out before translating: z.B., usw., d.h., bzw., e.g., i.e., asap …
- The quick-translate bubble reads numbers, compounds and abbreviations the same way.
- Fixed: a one-letter typo in a whole word (Schmeterling) is offered as *Did you mean Schmetterling*, not split into strange parts.

## 1.7 — 2026-11-25

- **Built-in dictionary**: about 14,000 common English and German words (7,800 English, 6,000 German) with their translations, and Persian words through them, are inside the app. The answer appears at once, also without internet; the translation service's fuller answer replaces it when it arrives.
- **Faster suggestions**: the first suggestions are translated while you type, so tapping one shows the result at once.
- **Quick-translate bubble** (Settings, *Over other apps*): a small button over other apps. Copy a word or sentence, tap the bubble, and the translation opens in a small panel. Drag it to the × to turn it off.
- **Swipe in Recent**: swipe a word to the right to save it, to the left to remove it (with *Undo*). Long press gives the same choices.
- **Saved words**: search (also in the translations, without typing umlauts) and sort (newest, oldest, A to Z, by level, hardest, next to review).
- **Listen & write**: the phone says a word or sentence and you write it. Saved words, words of a level (A1 … C1) or sentences; German nouns come with their article. Missed letters are marked; 5 at a time, then *5 new*.
- **Words from a text**: paste or share an English or German text, choose your level, and get its new words in dictionary form (ging → gehen, Häuser → Haus) with their meaning. Save the ones you choose to a folder in one step. Also from *Scan text*.
- **Backup and restore** (Settings): saved words, review progress, recent words and settings in one file. Restoring adds to what is on the phone and deletes nothing.
- **Tablets and landscape**: the translation and the meaning are shown side by side; other screens keep a readable width.
- **Feel**: a short vibration and a small animation for right and wrong answers, a card that turns over in Review, a soft fade for new results. Vibration can be turned off in Settings.
- **Introduction** on the first start: four short pages (also in Settings, *Show the introduction*).
- **Pictures for more words**: a German prefix verb without its own picture gets its base verb's (einschlafen → schlafen); verbs get the Wikipedia picture of the activity (öffnen → Opening, sleep → Sleep).
- Fixed: **Review** asked about every looked-up text, also typos and half-typed words. Now only real English and German words from Recent are reviewed (and saved words); a word removed from Recent is no longer asked, and old cards of words that were never reviewed were removed.

## 1.6 — 2026-11-24

- **Meaning in the word's own language**: what the word means, explained like a monolingual dictionary (English, German and Persian), with examples. Choose *Translation* or *Meaning* above the result; in *Meaning* mode the translation stays hidden until you tap *Show translation*.
- **Picture** for the word: a simple picture chosen to show the meaning (from Wiktionary / Wikipedia). Tap to enlarge.
- **Pronunciation practice**: listen to a real recording, record yourself, and play your recording to compare.
- **Word level** (A1 … C2) next to English and German words.
- **Synonyms, opposites and word family**, each word tappable.
- **Voice search**: say a word you heard; the other spellings that were heard are offered too.
- **Saved words**: the star saves a word; folders; export for Anki.
- **Review**: flashcards with spaced repetition for saved and looked-up words (Again / Good / Easy).
- **Grammar practice**: 5 questions per lesson or mixed up to a level, and *5 new questions* for another set.
- **Find the mistake**: which sentence is correct? 5 at a time, then 5 new ones.
- **Check a sentence**: write a German, English or Persian sentence; mistakes are marked with corrections and the matching grammar lesson.
- **Scan text**: take a photo or choose / share a picture; the text is read on the phone (no internet needed) and each word can be tapped to translate it.
- **Text size** setting and **widget colours** (as the phone, light or dark).
- Fixed: some German verb forms were not traced back to their infinitive (spielt → spielen).

## 1.5 — 2026-11-24

- **Grammar**: a new Grammar screen (book button at the top) with 60 German and 49 English lessons from A1 to C1 — explanation, table, examples with Persian and English/German translations, and related lessons.
- Explanations in Persian, English or German (button at the top right of the Grammar screen).
- Search lessons by topic in any of the three languages (Perfekt, passive, جداشدنی, trennbar) or by a word: fernsehen finds separable verbs.
- A looked-up word gets a **Grammar** button with its lessons (fernsehen → separable verbs, Tisch → articles and plural, went → irregular verbs).
- Fixed: German example sentences were often missing when the word was typed in lower case (tisch).

## 1.4 — 2026-11-24

- Phrasal verbs: for English verbs, a button shows the common phrasal verbs (give → give up, give in …) with their meaning and Persian translation. Tap one to translate it.
- German prefix verbs: German has no phrasal verbs, but prefix verbs do the same job (kommen → ankommen, mitkommen, bekommen …); they are listed the same way.
- Examples in every language: the Examples panel has a tab for the word and for its translations, so a German word also gets English example sentences.
- Short German words are recognised as German more reliably ("Haus" was sometimes taken for English).
- The plural article "die" is shown in a neutral colour (it is not the feminine "die").
- Fixed: the app could close after turning the phone or changing the theme.
- Suggestions while typing disappear when the keyboard closes.

## 1.3 — 2026-11-24

- German articles: every German noun shows der / die / das (in colour) and its plural, also in the meanings list and for a typed German noun.
- German words typed without ä, ö, ü or ß are understood: mude → müde, strasse → Straße, fuer → für.
- Word suggestions while typing, in all three languages (dicti → dictionary). Tap one to translate it.
- "Did you mean" now also works when the translation service has no correction (built-in word lists, all three languages).
- Better examples: proofread sentences first (recorded by people or written by native speakers), the meaning you looked up first, no near-duplicates.

## 1.2 — 2026-11-23

- **Examples**: a button under the result opens simple real sentences with the word (from Tatoeba),
  each with its translation into the other two languages.
- **Conjugation**: for verbs, a button opens the conjugation in German, English and Persian

- The update window shows the release notes as clean text.
- Update check also works when GitHub's API limit is reached.

## 1.1 — 2026-11-23

  If the service does not know the misspelled word at all, the corrected word is translated right away

## 1.0 — 2026-11-23

First release.

- Type a word in English, German or Persian and see it in the other two languages at once.
- The language is detected automatically; tap English, German or Persian to choose it yourself (e.g. "Gift").
- Shows the different meanings of a word, grouped by word type (noun, verb…).
- Home-screen widget: a search bar that opens the app ready to type; make it taller to see recent words.
- Select a word in any app and choose Trilingo from the menu; also works with Share and from a Quick Settings tile.
- Looked-up words are saved and work without internet.
- Listen to the pronunciation (when the phone's text-to-speech has the language) and copy with one tap.
- Google Translate by default, MyMemory as a backup; the other one is used automatically if one does not answer.
- Light and dark theme. Checks GitHub for new versions and installs them from inside the app.
