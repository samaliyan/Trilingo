# Changelog

## 1.2 — 2026-11-23

- **Examples**: a button under the result opens simple real sentences with the word (from Tatoeba),
  each with its translation into the other two languages.
- **Conjugation**: for verbs, a button opens the conjugation in German, English and Persian
  (e.g. habe → haben: ich habe, du hast, er hat …), with a tab per language. Works without internet.
  - German: present, simple past, perfect (with haben or sein), future, imperative; separable and
    inseparable verbs and "sich …" verbs.
  - English: present, present continuous, past, present perfect, future; irregular forms.
  - Persian: present, simple past, past continuous, present perfect, subjunctive, future, imperative;
    compound verbs (کار کردن) and verbs like برگشتن.
- The update window shows the release notes as clean text.
- Update check also works when GitHub's API limit is reached.

## 1.1 — 2026-11-23

- "Did you mean": when a word looks misspelled, the corrected word is shown above the results; tap it to translate it.
  If the service does not know the misspelled word at all, the corrected word is translated right away
  ("Showing results for …"). Misspelled words are not saved in the history.

## 1.0 — 2026-11-23

First release.

- Type a word in English, German or Persian and see it in the other two languages at once.
- The language is detected automatically; tap English, German or Persian to choose it yourself (e.g. "Gift").
- Shows the different meanings of a word, grouped by word type (noun, verb…).
- Home-screen widget: a search bar that opens the app ready to type; make it taller to see recent words.
- Select a word in any app and choose Trilingo from the menu; also works with Share and from a Quick Settings tile.
- Looked-up words are saved and work without internet.
- Listen to the pronunciation (when the phone's text-to-speech has the language) and copy with one tap.
- Google Translate by default, MyMemory as a backup; the other one is used automatically if one does not answer.
- Light and dark theme. Checks GitHub for new versions and installs them from inside the app.
